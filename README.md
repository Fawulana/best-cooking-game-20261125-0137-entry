"Sizzle & Serve" is a top-down lunch-rush cooking game. You run a bistro kitchen through a 3-minute shift: take ingredients from crates, chop at the board, grill patties from raw to golden (leave them too long and they burn), assemble dishes on the counter, and serve at the window before customers' patience runs out. Five original recipes unlock as tickets arrive faster and faster, successful serves build a combo multiplier up to ×3, and three hearts stand between you and a failed shift — finish with a 1–3 star rating. Sounds are synthesized with WebAudio and the whole kitchen is vector-drawn.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`node serve.ts` serves the game locally at http://localhost:8080 (the default port; pass `--port <n>` to change it).
